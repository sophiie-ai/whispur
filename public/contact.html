Contact the Whispur team

Whispur is an open-source project, so most conversations happen in public on GitHub. Here is the fastest way to reach us for each kind of question.

Bugs and feature requests

The best place for a bug report or an idea is GitHub issues. Search first in case someone already reported it. For a bug, include your Whispur version (shown in the About window), your macOS version, your Mac (Apple silicon or Intel), the speech and cleanup providers you use, and what you expected to happen. The Requests tab in Settings shows each provider call with its timing and error, which is often the quickest clue.

Pull requests are welcome too. See the contributing guide.

Security issues

Please do not report a security vulnerability in a public issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for the repository, or email us at whispur@sophiie.ai. Tell us the affected version and how to reproduce the problem, and we will reply as soon as we can.

Privacy questions

Whispur has no account and no backend server, so we do not hold your audio, transcripts or API keys. The privacy page explains exactly what the app and this website do with your data. If something there is unclear, ask us.

Everything else

For press, partnerships or anything that does not belong in a public issue, email whispur@sophiie.ai.
