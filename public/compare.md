# Compare Whispur

How Whispur compares with other ways to turn speech into text on a Mac. Every competitor fact comes from that product's own site, with sources on each page.

- [Whispur vs Wispr Flow](https://whispur.app/compare/wispr-flow) (24 November 2026): Cross-platform cloud subscription vs a free, open-source Mac app that transcribes on-device by default.
- [Whispur vs Superwhisper](https://whispur.app/compare/superwhisper) (24 November 2026): Two dictation apps with local models and custom prompts: one multi-platform with a Pro plan, one Mac-only, free and open source.
- [Whispur vs MacWhisper](https://whispur.app/compare/macwhisper) (24 November 2026): MacWhisper leads with file and meeting transcription; Whispur does live dictation only. When to pick each, or use both.
- [Whispur vs Apple Dictation](https://whispur.app/compare/apple-dictation) (24 November 2026): What the free, built-in macOS Dictation does well, and what Whispur adds on top: engines, cleanup, vocabulary, styles and history.
- [Whispur and Granola: different jobs](https://whispur.app/compare/granola) (24 November 2026): Different jobs: Granola for meeting notes, Whispur for writing by voice. Plus how Granola's voice typing compares.
